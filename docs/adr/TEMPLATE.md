# ADR-nnn — Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Product Owner (drew), CTO / Principal Architect
- **Supersedes:** —
- **Superseded by:** —

## Context

What forces are at play? What constraint, requirement or problem made a decision
necessary? State it so that a reader six months from now understands the pressure
without needing the conversation that produced it.

## Decision

What was decided, stated in the active voice and precisely enough to be checkable.

## Rationale

Why this option and not the alternatives that were considered. Name the
alternatives. A rationale that does not mention what was rejected is advocacy,
not reasoning.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what work this obliges. Include the
unwelcome consequences -- they are the ones a future reader most needs.
